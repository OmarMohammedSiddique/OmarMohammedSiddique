# Hi, I'm Omar 👋

I'm a final-year Informatics and Computer Science student at Strathmore University in Nairobi. I build and ship complete products: web and mobile apps on Next.js and Supabase, interactive 3D on the web, and security and forensics tools backed by written research. Most of what I make solves a real problem in Kenya and East Africa, such as M-Pesa payments, school transport, scam SMS and reforestation.

- 🛠️ **I build:** full-stack products with Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase (Postgres, pgvector, PostGIS), and mobile apps with Expo
- 🌐 **I make:** scroll-driven 3D experiences with three.js, GSAP and shaders, plus C++ and OpenGL graphics
- 🔒 **I study:** digital forensics, defensive security and applied machine learning
- 📄 **I write:** IEEE-format papers for my research projects

**In progress (private for now):** a school transport tracker for Nairobi operators with live van location and M-Pesa fees, a tree survival monitor that checks planted trees using free satellite data, an on-device scam SMS detector for English, Swahili and Sheng, and a landed cost calculator for importing tech to Kenya.

## Research

- **SensorTrace:** PRNU camera attribution, with experiment records and an IEEE-format paper
- **Tree Survival Monitor:** a monitoring design that makes free Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2 and CHIRPS data useful for checking young planted trees in Kenya
